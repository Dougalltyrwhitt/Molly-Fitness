Wellness

⌚ Apple Watch tips
  • Use the Workout app to log pilates, netball, sprints and yoga — pick the closest activity type and your Watch tracks heart rate and calories automatically.
  • Sleep is tracked automatically overnight — check the Sleep app in the morning, then pop a quick score in the Today tab here.
  • Steps and Activity Rings track themselves all day — just glance at the Fitness app on your phone and jot steps in here if you feel like it.
  • The Mindfulness app has short breathing sessions that pair nicely with your yoga / stretch day.
Apple doesn't let regular websites read Watch health data directly, so this app can't auto-pull it in — but the manual bits above take seconds.
